# Read Assist — product pitch

This film introduces Read Assist through a connected story: making time for a growing reading list, listening while doing something else, understanding a word, and settling into comfortable reading.

The screen footage comes from real Read Assist 0.3.2 Android emulator captures, including the recordings used for the existing walkthrough and a new capture for the listening sample. The new edit enlarges authentic details and changes the composition to match the explanation. Recorded actions play at their original speed; editorial crops and pauses do not introduce app controls or simulated interactions. Dictionary footage shows manual search inside the reader. It does not demonstrate a selection gesture.

The English narration is generated separately in post-production using Kokoro v1.0 and the `af_heart` voice. After “Open a PDF and press play,” the promotional narrator pauses for a dedicated listening sample. This sample uses the app’s genuine recorded speech and visible word highlights from the same continuous device recording. The source audio and video retain their original relative timing and speed, including brief gaps in the app’s recorded highlighting. The demonstration does not claim perfect word synchronization. A constant volume adjustment raises the app speech for clarity, with short fades at the excerpt boundaries. Its selected window, measured gain and fades are recorded in the build report. Outside this sample, source app audio is muted. The promotional narrator is separate from the app’s installed voice. The [voice credits](https://sainadh812.github.io/ReadFlow/PRODUCT_PITCH_VOICE_CREDITS.md) record sources, licenses and synthesis details. English captions and a downloadable soundtrack accompany the film. No music is added.
