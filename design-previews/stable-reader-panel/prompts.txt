OPTION A
Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high-fidelity design approval image for Read Assist Android reader.
Input image 1 is a reference for the REAL existing app, its warm paper palette, serif reading page, teal controls, Text/PDF segmented selector and up-chevron. It is a reference only; create a new polished concept board, do not replicate its cluttered readiness block.
Create a landscape 1536x1024 design board. Warm off-white background. Two large perfectly front-facing Android reader screens side by side with subtle thin device outlines, no perspective, no hands, no props. The screens fill most of the board and must have identical dimensions. A short title above them, small external state labels above each screen: left "Ready", right "Preparing audio". The app reading text and page viewport geometry must be pixel-aligned and identical between the two states. Both show the same top toolbar with back arrow, "A quiet space for thought", "Page 1 of 3", Aa, overflow menu; same cream paper, elegant readable serif paragraph; no new app branding or artwork.
Reading text verbatim: "Make room for a good read" then "Reading opens a quiet space for thought. A page gives us time to pause, notice a detail, and follow an idea at our own pace. Each sentence adds something new to the picture."
Use warm cream #F6F2E9, forest teal #246B61, dark ink #283B34, subtle sage. Crisp mobile UI, comfortable touch targets. Medium primary circular play button approximately 56dp, familiar previous and next track icons, up-chevron means expand settings. All existing controls must remain visible in both states. Preparing state may mute the play triangle but MUST NOT remove or move any buttons. Show the page and dock boundary at EXACTLY the same vertical location in both states. No progress percentage or fabricated time estimates. No pop-up, no toast, no readiness summary box, no large loading banner, no moving panel, no arrows inside the reading text. No explanatory labels inside the app beyond the specified status. This is a design concept, not a screenshot claim.
Title verbatim: "A · Steady dock"
Subtitle outside phones: "One fixed panel. One reserved status line."
Design A: An opaque full-width warm-white footer attached to the bottom edge, gently rounded top corners. Top row has centered Text / PDF segmented control, Text selected. Second row has five perfectly evenly spaced controls: "1×", previous icon, medium teal play circle, next icon, up-chevron. Third row is a small SINGLE fixed-height status slot centered below controls, always present: left screen reads "Ready to listen"; right reads "Preparing audio…". One tiny static hollow dot beside the right status, no dominant spinner. Both footer heights identical; changing status text cannot affect layout. Plenty of room around medium playback controls without oversized panel. Bottom Android gesture bar below footer. Small board footer outside phones: "The reading page stays in place."
