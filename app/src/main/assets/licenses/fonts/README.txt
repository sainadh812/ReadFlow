ReadFlow reader fonts

OpenDyslexic, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, Lexend, Literata, Source Serif 4,
and Noto Sans are bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1).
Each font's copyright statement and full license are in its own OFL.txt.
Embedded font metadata, including additional copyright statements, is preserved.
The fonts may be used in a paid app; they must not be sold by themselves.
Their license does not require the surrounding app to be open source.

The binaries are unmodified. Only filesystem names were changed to meet
Android resource naming rules. No subsetting, conversion or glyph edits were
performed. The five variable fonts provide their original weight variations.
OpenDyslexic includes separate original regular and bold files. System Serif
uses the device's built-in font and adds no bundled font binary.

provenance.json records immutable source revisions, source URLs, exact SHA-256
digests and font metadata. Google Fonts METADATA.pb files are retained where
available. No font download or third-party font service runs in the app.

These are appearance choices. ReadFlow does not claim that any font diagnoses,
treats, or universally improves dyslexia or another reading condition.
