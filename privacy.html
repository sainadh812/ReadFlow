Read Assist

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Developer: Oh My Pi
Privacy and support: xperimenting.ai@gmail.com

Read Assist is an Android document reader with text-to-speech and optical character recognition (OCR). It was previously called ReadFlow and uses the package name app.readflow. This policy describes how the app and its developer handle information.

Documents and reading on your device

You choose the PDFs, images and webpages to import. Imported content, extracted text, generated speech, reading progress and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Document recognition and speech generation run on your device. Your document content is not sent to model hosts for these operations. No Read Assist account is required. The app has no advertising SDK or Google Analytics SDK. Optional crash reporting is described below.

Internet connections and model downloads

Downloading voice, alignment and optional OCR models connects to the model hosts identified in the app, including Hugging Face, GitHub and their download infrastructure. Importing a webpage connects to the website you choose. Those services receive connection and request information, such as your IP address and the requested resource, under their own privacy policies. The app uses HTTPS for these requests. After models and content are downloaded, local reading and speech do not require a document-processing server.

See the Hugging Face privacy policy and GitHub privacy statement.

Google ML Kit

Read Assist includes Google's ML Kit text-recognition SDK. Google states that ML Kit processes input images and text on the device and does not send that input or recognition results to Google. ML Kit sends technical diagnostics and usage metrics to Google, including device and application information, installation identifiers, performance measurements, configuration, input/output sizes, events and error codes. Local document processing does not mean that the app and its SDKs never transmit data.

See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and ML Kit terms and privacy information.

Optional automatic crash reporting

Builds configured for Firebase Crashlytics offer an optional automatic diagnostics switch, off by default. Firebase is initialized at app startup only when the build is configured and you previously enabled this switch. Builds without that configuration cannot send Crashlytics reports. Declining diagnostics does not limit reading or any other app feature.

When enabled, Crashlytics sends crash, freeze and technical failure information to Google Firebase for the developer to investigate reliability problems. This includes stack traces, device and app information, application state, performance or memory measurements, and Firebase installation, session and Crashlytics identifiers. We do not set an account identifier or include Google Analytics.

For errors handled by Read Assist, we send a reduced technical summary without document text, document names, source URLs, audio or raw error messages. We do not attach your documents or audio to automatic reports. Automatic crash traces and native crash dumps can nevertheless contain incidental information from the app's runtime; they are not guaranteed to be free of all private content.

Changes to automatic reporting take full effect on the next app launch. Turning it off immediately stops Read Assist from submitting additional handled-error reports, but already queued work may finish and SDK crash handlers remain active until the process ends. We request deletion of unsent reports; this is not a guarantee that all pending reports have been removed. Reports still stored on your device may be sent if you enable reporting again. Turning the switch off or uninstalling the app does not remove information already received by Firebase.

Google says Crashlytics keeps crash traces, extracted native-crash data and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. Native minidumps are held temporarily for processing. Related Firebase services have their own retention rules. See Firebase privacy and retention information and Firebase's Android data disclosure. Contact the privacy email above with requests about reports received by the developer; do not send private document content just to identify a report.

Local error reports, benchmarks and support

The app stores issue reports and benchmark results locally. Reports can include document excerpts, source identifiers, device and configuration information, errors and generated speech. The local issue store holds at most 20 reports within a 24 MiB limit; older reports can be removed when these limits are reached. You can review, export or delete reports. An individual export may include retained audio; combined history exports exclude audio.

“Report a problem” lets you write a description and preview an email draft to xperimenting.ai@gmail.com. Including a reduced technical summary is a separate choice, off by default. That summary excludes document text, names, URLs, audio and raw error messages; your own description is included as written. No files are attached by this flow. You can open your email app or copy the preview, and choose whether to send it. Opening a draft or copying it does not send it. Your email provider handles the message and sender information under its own terms.

Production builds do not offer GitHub upload or GitHub token entry. Older prototypes and developer debug builds may retain that workflow: uploads require a configured destination and confirmation; reduced reports retain technical data and an installation identifier, and text excerpts require a private repository. Saved GitHub credentials in those builds are protected by Android Keystore. Original document files and audio are not attached to their GitHub uploads.

When you contact the developer or send a report, the developer receives the contact details and content you choose to provide and uses them to respond, investigate problems and maintain the app. Support correspondence and reports received by the developer are deleted within 90 days after the request is resolved. You can contact the privacy email above to request earlier deletion.

Copies exported to other apps or sent to another repository or recipient are subject to that destination's handling. Local deletion does not delete those copies.

Optional contributions

All existing app features are free. You may choose to support the developer using “Buy me a coffee” and the recipient UPI ID readassist@ybl. Contributions do not unlock features, usage, badges or priority support.

If you open a payment app, Read Assist passes the recipient UPI ID, the display label “Read Assist,” the selected INR amount and an optional-support note. You may instead copy the public UPI ID to your clipboard and enter it in your payment app. No document information is included.

Read Assist does not receive payment callbacks, verify payment status or store transaction records. It does not ask for or handle your UPI PIN, bank password or account-access credentials. Your payment app and the payer's and recipient's banks process transfers under their own terms and privacy policies. The developer may receive transaction information through the recipient's payment service. Depending on the payment provider, this may include the transfer amount, date, reference and payer details displayed by that provider. The developer uses this information to answer contribution questions and meet accounting or legal requirements. Payment records are kept only as needed for those accounting or legal requirements. Bank and payment-provider retention is governed by their own policies.

Check the recipient name, UPI ID and amount in your payment app before confirming. Returning to Read Assist is not confirmation that a transfer succeeded; check your payment app for its status. Use the support email above for contribution questions.

Storage, deletion and your choices

Deleting a document from the library removes its local content, associated speech cache and issue reports. You can delete other reports and downloaded models through their respective screens. Clear app storage or uninstall Read Assist to remove remaining local app data. Android backup and device transfer of app data are disabled. Delete files exported outside the app separately.

For information held by the developer, send a deletion request to xperimenting.ai@gmail.com and identify the report or correspondence concerned. Do not send bank credentials, PINs or unnecessary document content. Some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute. Other recipients and payment services manage their own records; uninstalling the app does not erase them.

Security

The app uses private device storage and HTTPS for its network requests. Firebase encrypts its reporting traffic in transit. This does not mean document storage is end-to-end encrypted or that exported files have the same protections. Review reports before sharing private content.
